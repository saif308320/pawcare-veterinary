About PawCare

A neighbourhood clinic with a big heart

PawCare Veterinary Hospital opened its doors in the heart of Kensington in 2016 with a simple belief: every pet deserves treatment as gentle as it is expert. What began as a two-room practice is now a full hospital trusted by thousands of families.

We combine modern diagnostic technology with old-fashioned compassion — taking the time to listen to you, understand your pet, and build care plans that fit real life.

  • In-house lab & imaging
